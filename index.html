White Nights

Author: Fyodor Dostoevsky

Read:time

Rating: 4.5/5

A beautiful, melancholic story about a lonely dreamer who falls in love with a woman he meets over four nights in St. Petersburg. Dostoevsky captures the pain of unrequited love and the comfort of fantasy with heartbreaking tenderness. The final scene, where the dreamer returns to his solitude, is devastating yet oddly peaceful.
