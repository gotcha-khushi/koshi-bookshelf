
# Koshi's Bookshelf

**Koshi's Bookshelf** is a personal digital bookshelf. I made it to keep track of my reading, rate books, and write reviews and thoughts about the book.

I like reading books that make me think, and I wanted somewhere to keep my reviews instead of forgetting everything after finishing a book. I also wanted to make a website with a design I actually liked, so I went with a vintage bookshelf look with colourful book spines and a paper-like background.

## What it does

- Shows my books as spines on a bookshelf.
- Lets me keep my own reviews, ratings, authors, and reading dates together.
- Has a light and dark theme.
- Clicking on a book shows its details and review.
- Includes an About Me page.
